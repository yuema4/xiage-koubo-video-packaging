# Repository conventions

- SKILL.md is the executable workflow; references/ holds detailed guidance.
- Keep README.md and README.zh-CN.md aligned when changing user-facing behavior.
- assets/landscape/ contains approved public landscape preview images and source/license notes; use portable English filenames.
- Do not commit raw footage, SRTs, local absolute paths, credentials, render caches, or large MOV exports.
- The creator approved publication of their likeness in these example composites on 2026-10-26. This does not authorize publishing unrelated personal assets.
- Preserve existing assets. Validate the skill, image links, and staged changes before publishing. Do not force push.
